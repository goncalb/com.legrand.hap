How this app connects

The Legrand gateway (Home + Control) is also a HomeKit bridge. This app pairs with it directly over your local network using the HomeKit protocol — the same way an iPhone would — and controls your switches, shutters and remotes without the Legrand cloud.

Nothing changes for the Home + Control app: it keeps working as before through Legrand's own channel.

Pairing for the first time
  1. Make sure the gateway is not already added to Apple Home (see below).
  2. In Homey, add a device → this app → any device type. The wizard lists Legrand gateways found on your network.
  3. Select the gateway and enter its 8-digit HomeKit setup code. You find it on the label of the gateway (XXX-XX-XXX), or in the Home + Control app under the gateway's HomeKit / Apple Home section.
  4. Pairing takes a few seconds. Afterwards the wizard shows your devices; tick the ones to add.

You only pair once. Adding more devices later skips straight to the device list.

"Already paired" / pairing refused

A HomeKit bridge accepts only one controller. If the gateway is already in Apple Home, it refuses new pairings and the wizard shows it as already paired.

Option 1 — remove it in Apple Home. Home app → Home Settings → Hubs & Bridges → the Legrand gateway → Remove Bridge from Home. This also removes the Legrand devices from Apple Home.

Option 2 — reset HomeKit on the gateway. Needed if it still shows as paired after removal, or was paired by a device you no longer have.

  1. Reach the reset button: on a gateway socket, unscrew the plate and the ring around the socket; on a gateway module it is on the front panel. It is marked with a small cogwheel — use a toothpick. The cover depends on your Legrand range (on Arteor, remove the decorative cover).
  2. Press and hold about 10 seconds while watching the LED: green → off → a brief orange/red flash. Release at that first flash. A short purple flash confirms the reset.
  3. Do not keep holding. Continuing past the first flash performs a full reset of the installation (all devices, rooms and settings). The HomeKit reset only clears the pairing and the gateway's Wi-Fi settings.
  4. Open Home + Control and reconnect the gateway to your Wi-Fi (the app guides you through it). Your devices and rooms are kept.
  5. Once the gateway is back online, pair it from Homey. The HomeKit pairing now lives on Homey — do not add the gateway to Apple Home again.
Gateway not found on the network

Discovery uses mDNS (Bonjour). If Homey and the gateway are on different VLANs or Wi-Fi networks, enable mDNS reflection on your router — or skip discovery entirely: enter the gateway's IP address in the Connection section above and give it a DHCP reservation so it never changes.

Device names, icons and modes
  • Devices arrive with the gateway's module names (e.g. "Switch 16"). Rename them in Homey; use Blink to see which wall module is which.
  • Icons are chosen when a device is added and can later only be changed from Homey's own icon gallery.
  • Changing a shutter's control mode (Aperture ↔ Orientable) in Home + Control is picked up automatically: the slat-tilt controls appear or disappear on the next refresh.
Starting over

To pair with a different gateway or after a HomeKit reset: remove the app's devices, uninstall and reinstall the app (this clears the stored pairing keys), then pair again. The gateway's HomeKit pairing must be reset too if it still remembers Homey.
